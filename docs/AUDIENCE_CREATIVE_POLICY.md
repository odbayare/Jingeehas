# Jingeehas Audience and Creative Policy

Updated: 2026-08-01

## Audience rules

- Start with Mongolia, age 25–65+, all genders, broad/Advantage+ audience.
- Do not target or infer weight, BMI, obesity, eating disorders, mental-health conditions, trauma, body dissatisfaction or assessment results.
- Do not create audiences from raw assessment answers, report dimensions, safety responses or sensitive product outcomes.
- Purchaser exclusions may use a generic confirmed-purchase event only.
- Retargeting may use generic website or funnel events only after privacy and audience-size review.
- Do not reuse LifePattern or another sensitive product's audience automatically.

## Creative rules

- No body shaming, personal-attribute assertion, before/after comparison, humiliation or guaranteed weight loss.
- Do not say or imply “та тарган”, “таны сэтгэлзүйн асуудал”, diagnosis or treatment.
- Present the product as a non-diagnostic self-understanding test evaluation.
- Avoid fear, fake urgency and unsupported outcome claims.
- Landing page, ad copy, CTA, price and commercial flow must agree.

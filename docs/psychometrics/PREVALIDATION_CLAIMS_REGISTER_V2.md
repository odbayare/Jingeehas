# Jingeehas prevalidation claims register V2

> **DESIGN PHASE ONLY**
> **NOT YET PSYCHOMETRICALLY VALIDATED**
> **NOT A CLINICAL OR PSYCHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS**
> **NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION PSYCHOMETRIC CLAIMS**

## Scope

This register governs V2 research, landing pages, checkout, ads, sample reports, support scripts, recruitment, presentations, and partner communication before claim-specific validation. It does not authorize changing the current production application in this documentation PR.

## Allowed before validation

Allowed only when accompanied by clear limitations:

| Claim area | Allowed wording/meaning | Required qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | “Монгол хэлээр шинээр боловсруулж буй асуултын сан” | Candidate items; not yet validated; rights review ongoing |
| Development | “Өөрийгөө эргэцүүлэх профайл боловсруулах судалгаа” | Research/development, not a released psychometric test |
| Research beta | “Урьдчилсан судалгааны хувилбар” | Scores/items may change; no personal clinical benefit |
| Non-diagnostic scope | “Онош тавихгүй” | Does not replace doctor, psychologist, or dietitian |
| Norm status | “Монгол хүн амын жишиг үзүүлэлт тогтоогдоогүй” | No percentiles or population comparison |
| Self-report | “Таны өөрийн мэдээлсэн сүүлийн үеийн туршлагыг асууна” | Recall and response biases apply |
| Construct intent | “Эдгээр сэдвийг хэмжих боломжийг судалж байна” | Do not say the instrument already measures them validly |
| Participation | “Оролцоо сайн дурын; хүссэн асуултаа алгасаж болно” | No impact on commercial access |

## Prohibited before validation

- “Validated psychometric test,” “баталгаажсан психометрийн тест.”
- Psychological or clinical diagnosis.
- Clinical cut-off, clinical risk category, or screening result.
- Population percentile, norm comparison, or “compared with Mongolians.”
- High/medium/low barrier or risk described as validated.
- “Scientifically proven personalized psychological cause.”
- “This is why you cannot lose weight.”
- Prediction of weight-loss outcome, kilograms, adherence, relapse, or treatment response.
- “Accurate if you answer honestly.”
- “The test identifies hidden habits/causes.”
- “Personalized method proven to work for you.”
- Any interaction explanation unless a released validated interaction model exists.
- “Research-based” or “international-method-based” when it implies the Jingeehas instrument itself has been validated.
- Diagnostic-equivalent wording for loss of control, body image, emotional eating, or food preoccupation.

## Do not pair safe words with unsafe implications

“Онош биш” does not cure an adjacent claim such as “your exact psychological cause.” “Preliminary” does not permit a percentile. “Research” does not mean validated. Review the whole consumer impression, not isolated sentences.

## Claim-to-evidence gates

| Proposed future claim | Minimum gate |
|---|---|
| Nine constructs are measured | Content process plus independent structural validation |
| Scores are reliable for individual profiles | Internal consistency, test–retest, precision, and intended-use review |
| Validated for Mongolian adults | Independent claim-specific validation in declared population/version |
| Percentile/norm comparison | Representative normative sample, weights, uncertainty, active norm table |
| Predicts behavior or weight outcome | Preregistered prospective independent predictive validation |
| Interaction insight | Powered development, independent replication, calibration, fairness review |
| Personalized starting direction works | Recommendation/intervention effectiveness evidence |
| Clinical screening/cut-off | Clinical reference standard, governance, regulation, and safety review |

## Approval workflow

Every asset must record:

- exact wording and channel;
- instrument/report version;
- intended audience;
- evidence gate claimed;
- scientific, safety/ethics, legal/consumer-protection, and product approvals;
- approval and expiry dates.

Any material instrument or evidence change reopens claims review. Until then, the allowed wording above remains explicitly prevalidation.

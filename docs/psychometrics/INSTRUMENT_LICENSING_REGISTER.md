## Required file for every licensed comparator

Archive:

- executed license/permission and date;
- exact instrument/version and language;
- approved paper/electronic mode;
- participant population and sample count;
- research versus commercial scope;
- translation/adaptation approval and linguistic-validation requirements;
- permitted item/report display;
- scoring/manual rights;
- retention and audit obligations;
- expiration/renewal;
- attribution language;
- contact and correspondence.

## Decision rule

If rights remain unresolved, do not administer, translate, reproduce, adapt, score, or embed the instrument. It may remain a literature reference only. Comparator scores must never be exposed in a commercial report unless the license specifically permits that use.

# Pilot V2.1 Limitations

## Required status

The instrument is AI-designed and AI-pretested. It is not human-reviewed, psychometrically validated, clinically validated, normed, diagnostic, or ready for public psychometric claims.

## Known limits

- Synthetic personas are software fixtures, not users or validation participants.
- Equal weights, direction transforms, missing-data rules, and report wording are implementation contracts, not empirical findings.
- Scores have no validated bands, percentiles, clinical cut-offs, risk levels, population comparisons, reliability estimates, or measurement-invariance evidence.
- Construct rankings are within one response profile only. Cross-construct scores are not assumed to share a validated metric.
- No interaction hypothesis is active.
- Context does not alter scores. Safety signals are excluded from scores and analytics and can stop ordinary interpretation.
- The research-quality response affects no profile score.
- Private lifecycle analytics cannot support psychometric validation.
- Mongolian clinical, ethics, language, target-user, accessibility, privacy, and psychometric human review remain required before any broader pilot or claim.

No production migration, deployment, public navigation change, payment integration, advertising, or merge is included.

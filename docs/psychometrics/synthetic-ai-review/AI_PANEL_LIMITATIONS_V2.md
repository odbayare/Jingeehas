## What this panel is

This is a governed AI pretest of candidate wording and review procedures. Synthetic expert-role agents apply prompt-defined lenses. Synthetic target-user personas generate structured hypothetical interpretations. Outputs can identify candidate defects worth human investigation and test whether review schemas, blinding, assignment, and adjudication workflows are operational.

## What this panel is not

AI agents are not human experts. They hold no professional license, research credential, ethical accountability, native-language identity, clinical judgment, or lived experience. Synthetic personas are not participants, respondents, patients, target users, or members of any population.

No output is human expert review, human cognitive interviewing, empirical validation, clinical validation, population evidence, or proof of cultural appropriateness.

## Shared model limitations

- All runs use one GPT‑5.6 model family, even though two runtime variants are distributed across runs.
- Language models may share training data, architecture, alignment, and response biases.
- Repeated contexts are not independent human raters.
- Shuffled order and prompt variants reduce direct carryover but do not create human independence.
- Agreement can be artificially high because the agents share learned language patterns and prompt framing.
- Disagreement can reflect stochastic wording rather than substantive scientific conflict.
- Synthetic consistency metrics cannot be called inter-rater reliability, expert agreement, content validity, or psychometric reliability.

## Persona limitations

- Persona diversity is prompt-defined and finite.
- Dimensions are combined to stress-test interpretation, not to estimate subgroup behavior.
- A persona description cannot reproduce literacy, fatigue, stigma, food access, caregiving, culture, socioeconomic constraints, or identity as lived.
- No real memory retrieval, judgment, response selection, distress, embarrassment, uncertainty, or nonresponse occurs.
- Selected responses are generated text artifacts and must never be scored, aggregated as a distribution, or treated as user evidence.
- The matrix avoids diagnoses and personal details, but it cannot prove absence of stereotype or representational harm.

## Mongolian language and culture limitations

- AI may miss regional vocabulary, cadence, implied moral tone, politeness, idiom, and differences in literal reading.
- Surface-natural Mongolian may still produce the wrong construct or response process.
- AI cannot replace Mongolian cognitive interviews across reading-comfort and geographic contexts.
- A plausible rewrite can inadvertently resemble a protected translation or shift the construct.

## Safety and ethics limitations

- No agent actually experiences distress.
- Flags for shame, judgment, or sensitivity are textual forecasts only.
- The panel cannot test whether consent, skip/withdrawal, debrief, or urgent-safety procedures work with people.
- AI safety agreement cannot authorize administration of sensitive items.
- No synthetic disposition overrides ethics review, lived-experience input, licensing, or clinician-approved safety boundaries.

## Measurement limitations

The panel establishes no:

- factor structure or loading;
- item discrimination or difficulty;
- internal consistency, omega, alpha, or item-total relation;
- test–retest stability;
- convergent, discriminant, criterion, or predictive validity;
- measurement invariance or fairness;
- population distribution, percentile, norm, band, or cut-off;
- clinical utility;
- response-process evidence from humans;
- treatment or recommendation effectiveness.

Synthetic selected responses are intentionally excluded from all prohibited analyses.

## Reproducibility limitations

Hashes confirm file identity and assignment/order integrity. They do not guarantee deterministic model reproduction. Model services can change, stochastic outputs may differ, and internal training/version details are not exposed. The manifest stores structured prompts and model identifiers but no private reasoning, token, credential, or API secret.

## Proper use

Use this panel to:

- prioritize wording for human expert review;
- identify likely ambiguity and cross-construct confusion;
- refine cognitive-interview probes;
- test response-scale alternatives;
- audit documentation governance and blinding;
- register disagreements that humans and empirical data must resolve.

Do not use it to retain an item definitively, launch a score, make a consumer claim, or replace any human or empirical validation gate.

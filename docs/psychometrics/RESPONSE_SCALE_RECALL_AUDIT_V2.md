# Jingeehas response-scale and recall-period audit V2

> **DESIGN PHASE ONLY**
> **NOT YET PSYCHOMETRICALLY VALIDATED**
> **NOT A CLINICAL OR PSYCHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS**
> **NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION PSYCHOMETRIC CLAIMS**

## Executive finding

A 14-day recall period is a defensible common pilot frame for observable episodes and recent barriers, but it is not equally natural for every proposed item. Frequency works best for occurred behavior and least well for perceived capability, generalized automaticity, and statements about consequences or plan availability. Construct validity currently justifies one explicit exception: eating self-efficacy should use a confidence response scale. Before adding further scales, rewrite items to fit frequency where doing so preserves the construct; introduce another scale only when cognitive and empirical evidence shows that rewriting would change the intended construct.

## Proposed administration frame

Default stem:

> **Сүүлийн 14 хоногт доорх зүйл танд хэр олон удаа тохиолдсон бэ?**

Default occurrence-frequency options:

0. Огт байгаагүй
1. Ховор
2. Заримдаа
3. Олон удаа
4. Бараг үргэлж

Self-efficacy stem:

> **Сүүлийн 14 хоногийн нөхцөлөө бодоход, доорх зүйлийг хийж чадна гэдэгтээ хэр итгэлтэй байсан бэ?**

Confidence options:

0. Огт итгэлгүй
1. Бага зэрэг итгэлтэй
2. Дунд зэрэг итгэлтэй
3. Их итгэлтэй
4. Маш их итгэлтэй

“Not applicable / ийм нөхцөл тохиолдоогүй” should be evaluated separately from “never.” A situation that did not occur is not evidence of zero reactivity or maximum capability.

## State-like constructs

Emotional eating, cue reactivity, subjective loss of control, and hunger/satiety experiences can fluctuate with current stress, sleep, medication, food availability, and schedule. Fourteen days gives recent behavior with less recall burden than 30 days. It should be interpreted as a recent profile, not a stable trait. Retest interpretation must record material contextual change.

## Trait-like constructs

Habit automaticity is intended as a learned process, but its items ask about episodes during the last 14 days. Scores will combine:

- whether the routine existed;
- how often its triggering context occurred;
- how automatic the response felt.

Before validation, compare:

1. common 14-day occurrence wording;
2. conditional wording (“Сүүлийн 14 хоногт энэ нөхцөл тохиолдсон үед…”);
3. general agreement/difficulty wording without a recall period.

Choose based on comprehension, factor coherence, test–retest, and prospective behavior—not convenience alone.

## Episodic behaviors and opportunity

For a situation-dependent item, distinguish:

- the situation occurred and the behavior never occurred;
- the situation did not occur;
- the person cannot recall;
- the person prefers not to answer.

Collapsing these into zero biases scores. Research administration should capture non-applicability without increasing burden excessively. Production scoring rules must be frozen only after missing/opportunity patterns are studied.

## Automatic habits

Frequency can over-reward frequent environmental exposure. Agreement with “this happens automatically” may better isolate perceived automaticity but introduces generalized self-belief and acquiescence. A difficulty scale is not appropriate because automaticity can be experienced as easy while still being a barrier. The choice is unresolved and belongs in cognitive and pilot comparisons.

## Body-image avoidance

Fourteen days is preferable to “usually” because body-image concern can change and because the intended report is a recent profile. However, opportunities differ by season, work, clothing, camera use, and social participation. The module should:

- avoid assuming that mirror/photo/gym behavior is universal;
- include “ийм нөхцөл тохиолдоогүй” during research testing;
- distinguish preference, modesty, pain, cost, access, and privacy from appearance-driven avoidance.

Frequency remains the best candidate scale if opportunity handling works.

## Implementation barriers

Implementation items require a referent: the personally chosen eating-related action or plan. Without one, frequency responses are uninterpretable. The introduction should name the referent and allow “одоогоор хэрэгжүүлэхээр сонгосон төлөвлөгөө байгаагүй.”

Difficulty might feel natural (“хэр хэцүү байсан?”) but risks converting objective context into a trait. Occurrence frequency better captures failures and recoveries across a defined attempt. IM03 (“minimum version was ready”) is a plan-asset state, so it may need a yes/no/degree formulation or rewrite into an occurred behavior.

## Item-level response mismatches

| Item | Mismatch | Preferred next test |
|---|---|---|
| EE02 | Perceived soothing consequence, not event count | Rewrite to an eating occurrence, or test agreement if expectancy is intentionally measured |
| EC04 | Exposure plus persistent thought in one frequency response | Separate exposure from persistence or condition on exposure |
| SE01–SE06 | Capability belief | Retain dedicated confidence scale |
| HA02/HA04 | Generalized automaticity language | Compare conditional frequency with agreement |
| IM03 | Availability of a fallback, not repeated event | Rewrite as use of fallback when needed or test degree/availability |
| AC01 | Instructional response, not construct response | Research-only fixed selection; excluded from scoring |
| AC02 | Meta-belief about answering | Remove; no response scale makes it a valid construct item |

## Why not use one universal agreement scale

Agreement encourages acquiescence, can blur whether an event occurred, and is poorly suited to a recent behavioral profile. It may be useful for automaticity but should not be adopted merely for uniformity. Likewise, one universal frequency scale should not be retained if it changes self-efficacy into “how often I felt confident.”

## Decision gate

Freeze response formats only after:

- two cognitive-interview waves compare stems/options;
- non-applicability rates are reviewed;
- category thresholds are ordered and adequately used in pilot data;
- factor models do not show dominant scale/method factors;
- test–retest and prospective criterion relationships are acceptable;
- Mongolian readers can consistently distinguish `Ховор`, `Заримдаа`, `Олон удаа`, and `Бараг үргэлж`.

No response format is validated at this stage.

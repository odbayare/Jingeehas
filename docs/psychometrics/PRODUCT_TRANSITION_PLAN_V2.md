# Jingeehas product transition plan V2

> **DESIGN PHASE ONLY**
> **NOT YET PSYCHOMETRICALLY VALIDATED**
> **NOT A CLINICAL OR PSYCHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS**
> **NOT READY FOR PRODUCTION PSYCHOMETRIC CLAIMS**

## Current product status

The current paid product is a deterministic, rules-based self-reflection/case-formulation assessment with safety routing and versioned reports. It is not a validated psychometric instrument. V2 is a separate research and design program; it does not silently replace, rescore, or reinterpret current users.

## Immediate temporary marketing restrictions

Until the relevant validation gate is passed, remove or avoid:

- “psychometric” / “психометрийн”;
- “validated,” “scientifically validated,” “найдвартай баталгаажсан”;
- “test identifies the psychological cause”;
- “research-based” when it can imply this instrument itself has validation;
- “hidden habit,” causal “why,” treatment matching, or prediction;
- “accurate if you answer honestly”;
- population comparison, percentile, norm, high/medium/low severity;
- clinical screening/diagnosis language;
- claims that interactions between answers are validated;
- claims to measure constructs not covered by multiple items.

Permitted interim framing:

> “Таны өөрийн мэдээлсэн хариултыг дүрэмд суурилан урьдчилсан сэдвүүдэд эмхэлж харуулах өөрийгөө эргэцүүлэх үнэлгээ. Онош болон баталгаажсан психометрийн хэмжүүр биш.”

This wording must undergo Mongolian copy and consumer-understanding review before use.

## Preserve current-user access

- Existing paid reports remain readable under their original questionnaire, scoring, and report versions.
- Do not rescore historical answers with V2.
- Do not overwrite or regenerate reports merely to change scientific classification.
- Preserve recovery, entitlement, deletion, and support paths.
- Show a version-specific limitation note where legally and operationally appropriate, without changing the historical evidence payload.
- V2 research participation cannot be required to keep existing access.

## Pilot recruitment

Recruit outside and within the existing audience without claiming benefit:

- publish a research invitation separate from the sales CTA;
- include unpaid/noncustomer recruitment to reduce paid-funnel selection bias;
- use quota/stratified recruitment guided by the protocol;
- state time, sensitivity, compensation, withdrawal, safety limits, and lack of personal clinical benefit;
- do not imply that pilot participants receive a validated result;
- avoid using discounts or report access as coercive consent.

## Informed consent

Use a separate research consent flow and record. Explain:

- purpose is instrument development;
- items and scores are preliminary;
- research is distinct from the commercial service;
- foreseeable sensitivity/distress;
- voluntary participation and skip/stop rights;
- safety routing limitations;
- data separation, access, retention, withdrawal, recontact, and future-use choices;
- whether anonymized data/code/results will be shared;
- contact and ethics route.

Consent for research, recontact, linkage, and future secondary use must be granular.

## Beta report language

Only after the beta gate:

- “урьдчилсан судалгааны профайл”;
- “энэ оноо хөгжүүлэлтийн үеийнх”;
- “эмпирик босго, хувь хэмжээ, онош биш”;
- “өөрийн хариултад тулгуурласан дотоод харьцуулалт”;
- confidence marked `provisional` or `limited`;
- no production-enabled interactions;
- no recommendation framed as proven to work.

Participants must see limitations before their scores.

## Release gates

### Gate A — research pilot

Requires expert content review, cognitive interviews, cultural/linguistic review, original-item rights check, ethics approval, consent, safety protocol, research/commercial data separation, preregistration, and frozen pilot version.

### Gate B — beta research report

Requires interpretable EFA, item reduction with coverage retained, initial reliability, response-quality review, no material safety/fairness failure, reproducible scoring, and explicit preliminary copy. Still not a validated commercial psychometric product.

### Gate C — validated instrument for declared scope

Requires independent CFA, prespecified reliability and test–retest evidence, convergent/discriminant evidence, relevant invariance/fairness review, immutable scoring/report versions, rights clearance, and independent scientific/claims approval.

Only claims directly supported by this evidence may be made. Criterion/predictive and norm claims remain gated separately.

### Gate D — norms and validated interactions

Norms require a separate representative normative sample. Each interaction requires adequate development, independent replication, incremental validity, calibration, and explicit production approval. One does not unlock the other.

## When the 9,900₮ paid promotion may resume

Paid promotion of the **current product** may resume only when:

- marketing clearly uses the current rules-based self-reflection classification;
- restricted claims are removed from landing, methodology, ads, checkout, sample report, and support scripts;
- consumers see material limitations before payment;
- no V2 or psychometric validation implication is borrowed;
- product, legal/consumer-protection, safety, and scientific owners sign a dated release checklist.

Paid promotion of **V2 as a psychometric profile** may begin only after Gate C and only for the validated language, version, population, purpose, and claims. Pricing itself does not confer validation.

## When “psychometric” may be used

Use “psychometric instrument/profile” only after Gate C, accompanied by the precise scope (for example, validated subscale structure and reliability in the declared Mongolian adult sample). Do not use it to imply diagnosis, clinical utility, norms, causal explanation, prediction, or treatment efficacy that was not separately established.

“Psychometric development study” may be used earlier to describe the research project, not the current consumer product.

## Claims prohibited before specific evidence

| Claim | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Measures nine constructs | Content evidence plus independent structural validation |
| Reliable individual profile | Reliability, retest, precision, and appropriate use review |
| Validated | Claim-specific independent validation |
| Compared with Mongolian adults / percentile | Representative normative sample and active norm version |
| Predicts adherence or weight change | Prospective criterion/predictive validation |
| Interaction insight | Independently replicated interaction model |
| Recommends what works for you | Intervention/treatment-matching evidence |
| Clinical risk or diagnosis | Clinical validation, governance, and appropriate regulated scope |

## Operational sequence

1. Freeze current classification and approved interim claim set.
2. Inventory every public claim and paid acquisition asset.
3. Preserve current-user access and version notes.
4. Complete rights/ethics/governance setup.
5. Run expert and cognitive stages.
6. Pilot frozen original item bank.
7. Reduce items and freeze the CFA/scoring specification.
8. Validate independently.
9. Conduct separate predictive/norm/interaction studies as required.
10. Approve exact claims and report templates.
11. Release a new immutable instrument version; never silently mutate the old one.

## Rollback

If post-release monitoring finds scoring, safety, fairness, or copy defects, stop new administrations of the affected version, preserve existing snapshots, disclose material impact, issue a successor version, and determine whether recontact/re-reporting is ethically required. Never rewrite historical reports in place.

## Final transition verdict

V2 remains a design and research program. Current production may be marketed only under a materially narrower rules-based self-reflection classification after claim review.
